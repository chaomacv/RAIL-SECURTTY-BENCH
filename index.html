About RAIL-SECURTTY-BENCH

RAIL-SECURTTY-BENCH is a domain-specific evaluation benchmark tailored for railway infrastructure security. Realistic infrastructure inspection requires robust anomaly detection across diverse structural scenes, yet existing evaluations often fail to capture the inherently unbalanced nature of actual field conditions. RAIL-SECURTTY-BENCH addresses this gap by providing a curated test dataset of 820 images spanning six critical categories—such as catenary poles, noise barriers and steel bridges—which naturally follows a long-tailed distribution to authentically reflect the real-world frequency of security-relevant anomalies.

Six Railway Infrastructure Security Scenarios

  • Catenary Pole:

    This category contains 11 images and is designed to evaluate the structural safety condition of railway catenary support systems. The annotated anomalies mainly involve foreign object attachments, such as bird nests, which may threaten the stability of railway power supply systems.

  • Environment:

    This category contains 128 images and targets external environmental safety hazards around railway infrastructure. Annotated anomalies include plastic films, accumulated garbage, and other intrusive foreign objects that may threaten railway operation when entering critical areas.

  • Noise Barrier:

    This category contains 227 images and concentrates on structural degradation in railway noise barrier facilities. The annotated anomalies include corrosion of barrier panels, corrosion of supporting columns, and aging or deterioration of mortar layers, reflecting long-term infrastructure damage under real operating conditions.

  • Power Tower:

    This category contains 91 images and focuses on safety risks associated with railway power towers. Annotated anomalies primarily include foreign objects such as bird nests that may interfere with power transmission safety, as well as loosened antenna bolts, enabling the evaluation of anomaly perception in complex elevated infrastructure environments.

  • Rail Track:

    This category contains 88 images and focuses on railway track integrity assessment. Typical anomalies include missing fasteners and cracked fasteners, both of which may weaken track stability and pose potential risks to train operation safety.

  • Steel Bridge:

    This category contains 275 images and represents the largest scene category in the dataset. Annotated anomalies include missing bolts, corrosion of steel coatings, coating peeling, and corrosion of bridge guardrails, all of which provide visual evidence of structural degradation and long-term service damage.
