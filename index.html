01 / Research Overview

One ecosystem, from visual evidence to verified anomalies.

RailMind and Rail-Security-Bench connect two complementary research components: an anomaly understanding and localization pipeline, and a domain-specific evaluation benchmark for realistic railway inspection.

◎
MODEL · RAILMIND

Multimodal anomaly understanding

A multimodal model identifies railway infrastructure anomalies from visual context. A detection model provides the localization stage for detected anomaly categories.

04 / Rail-Security-Bench

Six real-world railway infrastructure scenarios

The original Rail-Security-Bench evaluation set contains 820 images with a naturally uneven scene distribution. Each category emphasizes safety-relevant anomalies encountered during railway infrastructure inspection.
